Privacy Policy for SKY STRIKE

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SKY STRIKE ("the Game") handles information when you use the mobile game developed and published by TimesheetPro.

1. Information We Collect

SKY STRIKE does not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, phone number, or password to play the game.

The game may use third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob, which may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, and information about advertisements shown or interacted with, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

2. Advertising

SKY STRIKE may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners.

These advertising partners may use device identifiers and other information to provide, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to applicable privacy laws and their own privacy policies.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by the game or its third-party service providers may be used to operate the game, provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud, maintain security, and improve the game.

4. Third-Party Services

SKY STRIKE may use third-party services to provide advertising and related services. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

For information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the game and its service providers. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

SKY STRIKE is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by third-party service providers, including rights provided under applicable privacy laws.

You may also be able to control advertising personalization through the privacy and advertising settings available on your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of SKY STRIKE, you can contact the developer through the SKY STRIKE project repository on GitHub.
