Privacy Policy for SKY STRIKE

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SKY STRIKE ("the Game"), developed and published by Radwan Almasalmah, handles information when you use the mobile game.

1. Information and Data

SKY STRIKE does not require users to create an account or provide their name, email address, phone number, password, or other personal information in order to play the Game.

The Game may use third-party services that may process certain information from the device or about the use of the Game. The information processed may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location, IP address, technical information, and information related to advertising and app usage, depending on the services used and applicable settings.

2. Advertising

SKY STRIKE may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising providers.

Advertising providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location, and information about advertisements that are displayed or interacted with. This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising, subject to applicable laws and the provider's privacy policies.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy .

3. How Information May Be Used

Information processed by SKY STRIKE or its third-party service providers may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the Game.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Improve game performance and user experience.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, and security issues.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Game.

4. Third-Party Services

SKY STRIKE may use third-party services, including advertising and analytics services, to support the Game.

Third-party providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws. The handling of data by third-party SDKs is also subject to the policies and settings of those providers.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the Game and its service providers. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

SKY STRIKE does not require users to provide personal information to play the Game. We do not knowingly request personal information directly from children through the Game.

If you believe that personal information has been provided through the Game in a way that should not have occurred, please contact the developer.

7. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by third-party service providers, including rights provided by applicable privacy laws.

You may also be able to manage advertising personalization and related privacy choices through the settings available on your device or through the relevant third-party advertising service.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

SKY STRIKE does not require users to create an account. Information that may be processed by third-party service providers is handled according to the providers' applicable policies and retention practices.

If you have a privacy-related request concerning information processed by a third-party service, you may need to contact the relevant service provider directly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be updated when appropriate.

10. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of SKY STRIKE, you can contact the developer, Radwan Almasalmah, through the SKY STRIKE project repository on GitHub.
